## Reading the Market

1. We are in a clear downtrend. But price has been efficient with a lot of overlapping candle bars and wicks. It's a consistent downtrend with inconsistent inertia and low momentum, with an appearing sloping resistance level.

2. We then have have a break of structure. But not a normal one. This is a strong momentum candle that tears through and closes well before the previous swing low, printing a lower low. So we're still in the same downtrend, but this push down was atypical relative to prior market movement and momentum. It's also left behind an indecision/doji candle.

3. So then price continues down without ever retracing back to mitigate that inefficiency of the momentum candle. So on the larger retracement leg of the downtrend, we can antipicate for price to retrace back into that inefficiency or imbalance before pushing back down to continue the downtrend.

4. So we enter at the break of structure in the middle of the inefficienct box. Price couold also retrace deeper back into the the indecision candle, but we enter at the break of structure to be more sage and conservative. We stop loss just above the indecision block and target the most recent swing low.
