# Double Top Trend Reversal Example

![Double Top Trend Reversal Example](double-top-trend-reversal-example.png)

This is the basic and simplest entry for this strategy.

---

## Reading the Market

1. We are in a clear uptrend, over a substantial 12 day period (205 bars).

2. But then we see a slow in the trend - the double top. Price failed to make a higher high that could indicate that strength of buyers is slowing and even potentially a trend reversal (although we can't be too sure yet because it could go into sideways movement or even it could be a small pause before continuing to print higher highs and continue the trend).

3. But then we have a strong bearish monentum candle that tears through and closes below that previous swing low creating a change of character. Now we have enough evidence to speculate that we're looking at a trend reversal - the new change of character on top of the previously identified double top.

4. So then, we're looking for and anticipatin for the market to do one last "check" before continuing brearish move. That "check" is the retest we're looking to enter on, which in this case was the price of the previous swing low. This was the last price at which we can be confident majority of trades got facilitated and the market was balanced. 

5. So we enter at that change of character. Stop loss just above the boundary of the inefficency box and then target the previous support level.
